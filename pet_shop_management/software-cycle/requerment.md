# Project Requierments


### Login System
- email and password
- Session Based auth

### Product Information

- Store at least 10 pet shop products. Each product should contain Product ID, Product Name, Category 
(Food, Toy, Accessory, Medicine), Unit Price and Available Quantity.


### Display Product List
- Display all products in a formatted table. Show 'Out of Stock' if quantity becomes zero.


### Search Product
- Allow customers to search by Product 
ID or Product Name.


### Purchase Products
- Allow customers to purchase one or multiple products. Check stock before confirming the purchase.

### Multiple Purchases
- Continue purchasing until the customer finishes shopping.

###  Customer Information
- Collect Customer Name, Mobile Number and Membership Number.

### Membership Discount
- Maintain a predefined membership list. Members receive a 5% discount.


### Pet Type Discount
- If all purchased products belong to the same pet category (e.g., Dog or Cat), apply an additional 5% promotional discount.


### VAT Calculation

- Calculate 5% VAT after discounts and display the final payable amount.


### Shopping Cart Summary
- Display Product Name, Quantity, Unit Price, Total Price, Total Quantity and Grand Total.


### Inventory Update

- Update product stock after each purchase and display the remaining inventory.

### Pet Information
- Ask the customer for the pet type (Dog, Cat, Bird, Fish, Rabbit, etc.) and print it on the receipt.

###  Sales Receipt

- Generate a formatted receipt with shop name, 
customer information, pet type, purchased products, 
discounts, VAT and final payable amount.


###  Save Receipt
- Download option for Receipt.

### Sales History
- Append all completed purchases to SalesHistory model

### Payment Method

- Allow Cash, Card or Mobile Banking.
